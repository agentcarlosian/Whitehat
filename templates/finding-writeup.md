# Candidate finding

## Summary

Describe the demonstrated behavior and the exact affected boundary.

## Affected scope and version

Record the authorized asset, source/version identity, and tested configuration.

## Preconditions and reproduction

State controlled identities, required access, inputs, and minimal reproduction.
Do not include credentials, private user data, or unsupported hypothetical steps.

## Evidence and controls

Record normal behavior, changed variable, observed state, rejecting controls,
and evidence locators. Keep scanner claims distinct from demonstrated facts.

## Impact and limitations

Explain only demonstrated impact. Record alternative explanations and uncertainty.

## Duplicate analysis, mitigation, and cleanup

Record relevant existing fixes/advisories, a proposed correction, and restored state.

## Human review

Candidate disposition and decision about any external contact or submission.
